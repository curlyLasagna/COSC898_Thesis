\section{Use of DevContainers to standardize student's development environment}

\cite{valstarUsingDevContainersStandardize2020} highlights the benefits of DevContainers compared to pre-configured servers that the students remotely connect to. Pre-configured servers introduces friction with instructors as it requires them to collaborate with whoever manages the server. The process can be slow at times since the hosts manages multiple servers.

The students would also need to be on campus or connect via ssh. Having a good and stable connection isn't guaranteed and any drops can hinder the student's learning experience, especially in a graphical environment.

\section{CTF Archive}

To address the issue of students having to manually and imperatively reconstruct the CTF environment, \cite{guptaCTFArchiveCapture2025} introduced CTF Archive. CTF Archive is a platform to archive ready-to-use environments. These ready-to-use environments comes in the form of docker images that can be easily redeployed
